THE ATELIER PROTOCOL

Four Pillars of Bengali Satorial Honor

01 / FIBER SANCTITY

Unblended Wefts

We pledge zero polyester or viscose infiltration. Each thread is tested by water-drop and flame verification before entering the Dhaka cut room.

02 / HUMAN WAGES

Guild Equity

Our master weavers receive triple prevailing regional handloom rates, with medical support and university stipends for artisans’ daughters.

03 / PRECISION CUT

Floating Canvas

Collars are curved by iron heat steam rather than stiff synthetic adhesives. The garment molds to your physical form through the seasons.

04 / LIFELONG CARE

Heirloom Restorations

Every Oi Dulavai piece includes complimentary lifetime seam re-alignment, dry press servicing, and gold bullion re-polishing at our Banani atelier.
