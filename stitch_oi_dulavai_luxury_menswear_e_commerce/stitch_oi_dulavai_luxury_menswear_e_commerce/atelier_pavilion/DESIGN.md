## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an architectural, editorial luxury commerce paradigm for heritage South Asian menswear, tailored for discerning modern patrons, wedding parties, and sartorial collectors. The brand identity balances regality with clinical modern minimalism—rejecting ornamental clutter in favor of disciplined structural gridlines, tactile textile metadata, and theatrical art direction.

The design philosophy is governed by two complementary spatial moods:
- **Daylight Atelier:** Imbued with raw tussar silks, bleached muslin, warm sandstone, and artisanal brass. It serves everyday luxury, kurtas, Nehru vests, and daytime ceremony curation with an airy, gallery-like restraint.
- **Royal Midnight Pavilion:** A dramatic, nocturnal immersion evoking velvet sherwanis, bespoke zardozi, and royal soirees. Grounded in obsidian and deep lapis-tinted shadows punctuated by liquid champagne gold and deep emerald accents.

Across both atmospheres, the visual language relies on razor-sharp contours, editorial contrast, disciplined hairline grid boundaries, and deliberate micro-typography. The interface feels less like an off-the-rack web shop and more like a private salon fitting with technical atelier documentation.

## Colors

The system uses a contextual dual-palette architecture. Interfaces default to the **Daylight Atelier** mode, with dynamic or route-based transitions into the **Royal Midnight Pavilion** for nocturnal collections, bespoke evening couture, and the wedding concierge suite.

### Daylight Atelier Palette
- **Canvas Base (`#F7F4EE`):** Warm Alabaster Linen. A living, organic ground with subtle organic warmth.
- **Surface Elevation (`#EBE5D8`):** Safari Sandstone. Used for cards, sidebars, and tonal background stepping.
- **Primary Ink & Text (`#161412`):** Deep Espresso Charcoal. Delivers absolute clarity without the sterility of pure black.
- **Muted Text / Secondary Labels (`#6E655B`):** Warm Slate. Editorial metadata, captions, and dimensional metrics.
- **Primary Accent (`#B8893A`):** Antique Brass / Craft Gold. Used for craft insignias, active toggles, badge borders, and gilded moments.
- **Editorial Alert / Accent (`#8B2626`):** Terracotta Rust. Reserved for bespoke callouts, low inventory markers, and ceremonial accents.
- **Hairline Structural Border (`#DDD5C7`):** Warm Stone. 1px borders enforcing the geometric framework.

### Royal Midnight Pavilion Palette (Dark Mode Token Equivalents)
- **Pavilion Canvas Base (`#070C14`):** Deep Obsidian. Total black with an imperial navy undertone.
- **Pavilion Surface Layer (`#0B1320`):** Midnight Navy. Elevated modules, floating drawers, and architectural facets.
- **Pavilion Primary Accent (`#D8B467`):** Champagne Gold. High-clarity metallic for typography highlights, active states, and focal actions.
- **Pavilion Jewel Contrast (`#133327`):** Royal Emerald. Used for bespoke concierge badges, certified weave tags, and private client status indicators.
- **Pavilion Hairline Border (`#1C2638`):** Chiseled Obsidian Border. Subdued 1px definition for cards and structural separators.
- **Pavilion Primary Ink (`#F7F4EE`):** Warm Alabaster Linen text inverted for effortless high-contrast reading.
- **Pavilion Muted Text (`#8E98A8`):** Pale Mist Slate.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy orchestrates a tension between old-world royal prestige and cutting-edge digital precision:

- **Editorial Serif (`Bodoni Moda`):** Delivers stately grandeur across major hero statements, collection titles, and runway storytelling. The extreme thick-thin stroke contrast brings an unmistakably high-fashion, print-editorial aura. Always typeset with controlled negative tracking in large sizes.
- **Architectural Sans-Serif (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Handles core readable text, descriptions, navigation, and user actions. Clean geometry and open apertures provide frictionless legibility against tactile backgrounds.
- **Technical Monospace (`JetBrains Mono`):** Introduces atelier authenticity. Used exclusively for garment GSM metrics, weave origin coordinates, yarn counts, SKU serials, pricing, and sizing dimensions. It reads like a luxury garment tag stamped at an artisanal mill.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors an architectural gallery plan: strict columns, prominent 1px hairline segmentations, and deliberate whitespace framing dense garment data.

### Grid Anatomy
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** 1440px max-width container, 3rem margins, 1.5rem gutters. Layouts partition cleanly into 6-column split-screen editorial views, 4-column retail showcases, and 3-column bundle builders.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Fluid container, 2rem margins, 1rem gutters. Retail cards collapse to 2 columns with fixed edge-pinned action panels.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Fluid container, 1.25rem canvas margin, 0.75rem gutters. Product grids convert to full-bleed edge-to-edge scroll viewports with hairline dividers between items.

### Rhythm Rules
- Every content cluster aligns to an explicit 4px baseline grid.
- Dense technical data (GSM, yarn origin, measurement grids) is spaced tightly using `space-xs` and `space-sm` inside boxed cells.
- Visual storytelling (editorial lookbooks, campaign narratives) expands drastically using `space-xl` and section gaps of up to 6rem, granting garments breathing room.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately rejects heavy, generic drop shadows. Depth is communicated through structural layering, hairline contrast outlines, and luminous edge highlights.

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** The canvas base (`#F7F4EE` or `#070C14`).
- **Level 1 (Structural Inset / Tile):** Surface shifts to Sandstone (`#EBE5D8`) or Midnight Navy (`#0B1320`), bounded by a 1px solid hairline (`#DDD5C7` or `#1C2638`). No shadow.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active States):** The hairline border transitions to Antique Brass (`#B8893A`) or Champagne Gold (`#D8B467`). A micro-elevation shadow is applied: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(22, 20, 18, 0.06)` in Daylight, or `0 4px 24px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)` in Pavilion.
- **Level 3 (Interactive Hotspots & Floating Modals):** Drawers, concierge overlays, and fabric inspectors float with crisp backdrop filters: `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over a 90% opaque background. Boundary edge is defined by a dual rim: a 1px solid border accompanied by an inner hairline inset (`inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(216, 180, 103, 0.2)` in dark mode).

## Shapes

The shape geometry is intentionally severe, architectural, and tailored. 

- All buttons, product tiles, input containers, image viewports, and drawers feature sharp `0px` corners, reflecting the precision cut of luxury menswear suiting and bespoke shears.
- A maximum corner radius of `2px` is permitted solely on ultra-small micro-indicators (such as interactive garment hotspots or live status dots) to prevent visual aliasing on high-density displays.
- Visual containers rely on crisp 90-degree intersections, geometric border grids, and hairline intersections rather than organic soft shapes.

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Atelier Button:** Solid `#161412` fill (or `#D8B467` in Pavilion mode) with opposing high-contrast text. Rectangular, 0px radius, 48px height. Typeset in `label-caps` (all-caps tracking: `0.15em`). On hover, smoothly transitions background to Antique Brass (`#B8893A`) with zero elevation jump.
- **Secondary Hairline Button:** Transparent fill with a 1px border (`#161412` or `#D8B467`). Text matches border color. Hover inverts the fill entirely.
- **Text Link CTA:** Typeset in `label-caps` with an underline positioned 4px below baseline. Hover triggers an animated underline expansion from left to right.

### Retail Cards with Inline Size Selection
- Rectangular card bounded by a 1px hairline border. The imagery uses a fixed 4:5 editorial aspect ratio.
- The card base contains the editorial garment title (`headline-md`), fabric origin in `label-caps`, and the retail price in `spec-code`.
- **Inline Size Strip:** Hovering (or tapping on mobile) slides up a docked 36px high segmented bar at the base of the image. Sizes (38, 40, 42, 44, 46, Bespoke) sit in sharp hairline-divided cells. Clicking an available size immediately stages the item for checkout without page navigation, displaying instant stock confirmation via `spec-code-sm`.

### Interactive Garment Hotspots
- Subtle 16px circular focal rings placed across runway lookbook imagery.
- Constructed with a 1px brass outline and a centered 4px dot.
- Hovering or tapping expands a docked glass card displaying macro textile specs: Weave density (e.g., *Raw Matka Silk 340 GSM*), hand-embroidery hour count, and direct "Add Component to Bundle" action.

### Textile & Spec Chips
- Compact, flat structural rectangles with a 1px border.
- Font is strictly `spec-code-sm`. Used for raw fiber metrics: `100% TUSSAR SILK`, `ZARI THREAD 24K`, `DRY CLEAN ONLY`.
- Selected state turns the chip to solid dark fill with crisp inverted monospace text.

### Form Inputs & Concierge Fields
- Minimalist baseline or boxed cells. 1px hairline border that transitions from Warm Stone to Craft Gold on focus.
- Floating labels in `label-caps`. 
- Input values render in clean sans-serif (`Plus Jakarta Sans`), while bespoke measurement entries (chest, sleeve, waist, shoulder drop) render in `spec-code`.

### Bespoke Concierge & Bundle Builder
- A dedicated 3-column split interface allowing grooms and patrons to curate coordinated ensembles (Kurta + Bundi Vest + Churidar + Dupatta + Footwear).
- Left: Dynamic composite silhouette. Center: Active garment fabric customization and monogramming. Right: Running itemized spec ledger with live weight calculations and heirloom box packaging selection.
- All structural boundaries inside the builder are marked by 1px hairline borders that eliminate all ornamental UI noise.
