Textile Provenance & Archive

The Living Heritage of Rajshahi Pit-Looms & Zardozi Bullion

Every yard of this imperial silk is spun from wild sericulture cocoons nurtured along the Padma river basin, then painstakingly hand-embroidered by generations of Old Dhaka karigars.
PHASE 01 14 WEEKS REELING

Wild Tussar Sericulture

Cultivated in forested riverine banks. Unlike industrial spun yarn, wild Tussar filaments maintain deep porous micro-tubules that insulate against Bangladesh humid heat while radiating an amber-gold luster.

Padma River Basin Archive
PHASE 02 78 PIT-LOOM HOURS

Dense Slub Architecture

Woven on subterranean wooden floor-pits where ambient soil humidity maintains ideal tensile thread strength. Produces our signature heavy 320 GSM body that refuses to collapse or cling.

Rajshahi Guild Looms
PHASE 03 42 ZARDOZI HOURS

24K Bullion Couched Zari

Executed with genuine gold-plated metal purl wires along the bandhgala collar, hidden placket, and sleeve barrel cuffs. Hand-sewn with silk threads to avoid skin irritation.
